Ion Run Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Ion Run is a game made by Thomas McLarney ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We keep it to the minimum the game needs to work.

What we collect

  • An anonymous player ID. When you first open the game it signs in anonymously (through Google Firebase) to get a random ID. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or any account.
  • Your weekly best score. Your best score each week is stored with that ID so the weekly leaderboard can rank it and award prizes to the top three.
  • Prize claims. If you finish in the top three, we record that your ID claimed that week's prize so it is only given once.

Your progress (best score, cores, unlocked trails and skins, and settings) is saved on your device only.

What we don't collect

  • No names, emails, contacts, photos, location or microphone.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.

Purchases

Core packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play. They handle payment. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation of which pack was bought, so we can add the cores.

Who processes the data

Scores and player IDs are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Google may process technical data, such as your IP address, to provide that service. See Firebase's privacy information.

How long we keep it

Weekly scores and prize claims are kept only as long as needed to run the leaderboard. Deleting the game removes everything saved on your device and leaves your old anonymous ID unused.

Children

Ion Run collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices
