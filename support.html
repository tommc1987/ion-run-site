Ion Run Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email tommclarney@hotmail.co.uk and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to flip your orb's charge between + and −. Pylons on the walls pull you in when your charge is the opposite of theirs, and push you away when it's the same. Use that to steer through the gap in each barrier.

I bought cores but didn't get them

Make sure you're connected to the internet, then close and reopen the game. A completed purchase is added the next time the game starts. If they still haven't arrived, email us with roughly when you bought them. Refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Yes. Your progress (cores, unlocks, best score) is saved on your device only, so deleting the game removes it.

How does the weekly leaderboard work?

Your best score each week (Monday to Sunday, UTC) goes on the leaderboard. When the week ends, the top 3 win bonus cores, which are added the next time they open the game. No account is needed.
